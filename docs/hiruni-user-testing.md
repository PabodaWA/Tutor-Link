# Booking & Scheduling — User Testing

Owner: Hiruni  
Feature scope: availability, booking, rescheduling, cancellation, and reminders

## Test setup

- Test with at least three participants who did not build the feature.
- Use one verified tutor account and one learner account.
- Ensure the tutor has at least two future availability slots.
- Ask participants to think aloud. Do not guide them unless they are blocked.

## Tasks

1. As a tutor, publish a 60-minute lesson time for next week.
2. Publish the same time as recurring availability for four weeks.
3. Edit one open availability slot and then remove it.
4. As a learner, find a tutor and request a lesson.
5. As the tutor, find the request and confirm it.
6. As the learner, enable the lesson reminder.
7. Reschedule the lesson to another available time.
8. Cancel a booking and provide a cancellation reason.
9. Find the cancelled booking and identify its refund status.

## Post-test questions

1. Was it clear whether a booking was pending, confirmed, or cancelled?
2. Did the weekly calendar help you understand your schedule?
3. Was editing recurring availability understandable?
4. Did the booking confirmation contain enough information?
5. Was the two-hour cancellation policy visible before cancellation?
6. What was the most confusing part of the flow?
7. What would you change first?
